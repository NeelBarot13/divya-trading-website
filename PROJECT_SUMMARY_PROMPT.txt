Design, build, and deploy a complete, production-ready B2B E-Commerce Catalog & Quotation Management Web Platform for 'Divya Trading Co.' (Ahmedabad, India) - a precision manufacturer and global exporter of textile rotary printing and processing machine spare parts since 1997.

CORE ARCHITECTURAL SPECIFICATIONS:

1. B2B NO-PRICE CATALOG & MOBILE RESPONSIVE UI:
   - Precision parts for Stork RD-3/RD-4, Stormac, Pegasus, Ichinose, Reggiani, Harish, Zimmer, Stovec.
   - Prices are HIDDEN publicly. Customers request formal price quotations per repeat size and volume.
   - 2-Tier Description System:
     * Short Description: 1-2 concise lines shown on homepage featured cards and catalog grid cards.
     * Long Description: In-depth technical description shown on the single product detail page.
   - Stock Status: Badges and 1-Click updates for 'In Stock', 'Out of Stock', and 'Made to Order'.
   - 1-Click Active/Deactivate: Disable any product from live public visibility with instant async toggle.
   - Mobile & Tablet Optimization: Responsive collapsible navigation, fluid product cards (1-2 cols on mobile), and touch-friendly targets.

2. CUSTOMER PORTAL, AUTOFILL & MY QUOTES DASHBOARD:
   - Registration & Login (/register, /login) with automatic past guest inquiry linking by email.
   - Global Customer Detail Autofill: Pre-fills customer name, company, email, phone, and country across all inquiry forms and Contact Us page.
   - Contact Us Product Selector: Dropdown on Contact Us page allowing selection of specific spare parts.
   - Header Navigation:
     * Guest View: Clean, uppercase 'LOGIN / SIGNUP' menu text matching other navbar links.
     * Logged-in View: Sleek circular profile button with user initials and dropdown (My Quotes & Status, Request New Quote, Sign Out).
     * Complete Removal of Admin Links: Zero mention or link to admin panel on the public frontend.
   - Guest Inquiry Modal: Pop-up prompt suggesting account creation to track quotes with real-time status and live chat, with 'Continue as Guest' fallback.
   - 'My Quotes' Dashboard (/my-quotes):
     * Metric counters: Total Inquiries, Under Review, Quotation Ready, Completed.
     * Live status badges (Under Review, Quoted, Confirmed).
   - Quotation Details & Two-Way Discussion Thread (/my-quotes/<inquiry_number>):
     * Official quotation summary card (price amount, dispatch timeline, payment terms, validity).
     * Live interactive discussion thread between customer and DTC sales engineering team with email alerts.

3. DUAL QUOTE REQUEST FLOWS:
   - Single-Product Quick Inquiry: Modal pre-filled with product name, part number, repeat size, and quantity.
   - Multi-Product Quote List Drawer: Side drawer that collects multiple parts into a combined inquiry submission.

4. FULL-FEATURED ADMIN PANEL (/admin):
   - Inquiries & Quotation Preparation (/admin/dashboard):
     * Review submitted RFQs, input formal quote price (e.g. Rs. 1,45,000 / $1,750 USD), lead time, payment terms, and validity.
     * Direct reply in the two-way customer discussion thread with instant email alerts.
     * CSV Export of inquiries with one click.
   - Product & Stock Manager (/admin/products):
     * Add/Edit products with 2-tier descriptions, part numbers, machine make, category, photos, and specs.
     * 1-Click toggles for live activation and stock status directly on the data table.
   - Customer Accounts Manager (/admin/customers):
     * Directory of all registered textile mills, contact phone/WhatsApp, and total quotes submitted.
   - Team Roles & Password Management (/admin/users - Super Admin Exclusive):
     * Super Admin can assign access roles: 'Super Administrator', 'Sales & Quotes Manager', 'Catalog & Product Editor'.
     * Super Admin can view team password hints/values and update any team member's password directly.
   - Website CMS & Settings (/admin/settings):
     * Tabbed controls for Hero Title/Subtitle, Brand Compatibility Pills, 4-Product Showcase limit, 6 Value Pillars, Stats ribbon, and SMTP email settings."

DATABASE MODELS:

- InquiryItem: id, inquiry_id, product_id, product_name, part_number, quantity, notes
- InquiryMessage: id, inquiry_id, sender_type, sender_name, message, created_at
- AdminUser: id, username, email, password_hash, view_password, role ('superadmin', 'sales_manager', 'product_editor'), is_active
- SiteSetting: id, key, value, description
