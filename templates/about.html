Our Heritage

Over Two Decades of Manufacturing Excellence

DIVYA TRADING CO. SINCE 1997 has grown in strength to emerge as one of the reputed manufacturers and exporters of various spare parts for textile printing and processing machines.

We offer a complete range of high-precision replacement parts for STORMAC, STORK (RD-3 & RD-4), ICHINOSE, REGGIANI, HARISH, ZIMMER PRINTING, STOVEC PRINTING MACHINE, and Stenter Processing Machines.

With over two decades of experience, we have today emerged as a leader in quality products in our specialty of operation. Our reputation is based on the performance and reliability of our world-class precision products.
