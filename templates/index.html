FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Rotary Printing Spare Parts — Questions & Answers

Where can I buy precision Rotary Printing Spare Parts in India?

Divya Trading Co. (Ahmedabad & Surat, Gujarat) is one of India's leading manufacturers and global exporters of precision rotary printing spare parts. Operating since 1997, we manufacture complete replacement components including screen head rings, repeat gears, colour pumps, and squeegee blades with full in-house CNC machining and dynamic balancing facilities.

Are your spare parts compatible with Stormac and Laxmi rotary printing machines?

Yes! We manufacture 100% OEM-compatible spare parts for both Stormac and Laxmi rotary printing machines, as well as Stork, Zimmer, Reggiani, Harish, and Ichinose. Our parts meet or exceed original equipment tolerances to ensure accurate pattern registration and maximum service life.

What repeat sizes are available for rotary printing spare parts?

We manufacture screen heads and repeat gears for all standard repeat circumferences: 640mm (64R), 819mm (81.9R), 914mm (91.4R), and 1018mm (101.8R). We also provide custom reverse-engineered repeat parts manufactured to customer specifications or physical samples.

Do you export rotary printing machine spare parts internationally?

Yes, Divya Trading Co. regularly exports precision textile printing machinery spares to mills in Bangladesh, Turkey, Egypt, Indonesia, Vietnam, Pakistan, and African textile printing hubs. All export consignments are packed in reinforced export-grade wooden crating with quick dispatch by air or sea.
