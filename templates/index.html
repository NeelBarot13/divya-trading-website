COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I buy precision Rotary Printing Spares in India?

Divya Trading Co. (Ahmedabad & Surat, Gujarat) manufactures and exports complete rotary printing replacement components including screen head rings, repeat gears, colour pumps, and squeegee blades since 1997.

Are parts compatible with Stormac and Laxmi machines?

Yes, we manufacture 100% OEM-compatible spare parts for Stormac, Laxmi, Stork (RD-3/RD-4), Zimmer, Reggiani, Harish, and Ichinose meeting strict original manufacturer tolerances.

What repeat sizes are available?

We supply parts for all standard repeat circumferences: 640mm (64R), 819mm (81.9R), 914mm (91.4R), and 1018mm (101.8R), plus custom repeat sizes per client CAD drawings or samples.

Do you export spare parts internationally?

Yes, we regularly export textile printing machinery spares to Bangladesh, Turkey, Egypt, Indonesia, Vietnam, and African textile printing hubs with fast air and sea dispatch.
